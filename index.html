About

Engineer by training, seller by craft.

I started on refinery floors, commissioning control systems, analysers and fire & gas safety systems. I moved into running a large fuel retail business, and then into specification and pre-sales for smart building products at Schneider Electric and Legrand.

That path taught me to speak both languages: the engineer's and the buyer's. I work with architects, MEP consultants and developers to get products specified early, and I translate technical detail into business value that decision-makers act on.

Now I'm focused on what AI changes for pre-sales: discovery, demos, solution design and ROI, done faster and better.
